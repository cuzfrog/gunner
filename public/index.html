                      About Gunner

                      I wrote this tool to help me understand the dynamics of ship fighting and boost my confidence in a fight. Just like you, I love this game.

                      Kinematics

                      Ships move with mass and inertia. Transversal, radial, and angular velocity describe ship motion. Acceleration is exponential.

                      Velocity

                      Speed

                      Max velocity is hull base speed × propulsion bonus (afterburner / MWD) × skill modifiers; overloading boosts it further.

                      Propulsion

                      Hit chance

                      Hit chance is 0.5^(tracking term + range term), using angular velocity, turret tracking, signature, and distance past optimal over falloff.
