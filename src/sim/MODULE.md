# sim

Engagement simulation domain: ship reactive and predictive autopilot steering, the EVE-style dynamics engine (mass/inertia exponential velocity tracking), two-body kinematics, the EVE-style hit chance model, and the fixed-state simulation stepper. `EngagementEngine` is the module's orchestration entry point: it owns `reset`, `update`, and `step`, composes the frame via `EngagementFrameComposer`, runs the `DefenseSimulator`, and emits `EngineView` updates and `shipDestroyed` events through its `EngineEvents` surface. Sub-simulators (`Simulation`, `DroneSimulator`, `MissileSimulator`, `DefenseSimulator`, `WeaponClock`, `LockClock`) are engine-internal — they are constructed inside the `SimWorldFactory` and consumed by the engine, never imported outside `src/sim`. `dynamics.ts` is module-internal: its `timeConstant` and `integrateShip` helpers have no cross-boundary exports. `weaponDamageAssessor.ts` is the single source of truth for count-scaled volley assembly: `computeBaseVolley` applies the weapon count exactly once (`damagePerShot * count` for turrets/drones, `damagePerMissile * launcherCount` for missiles), and `assess` produces the full `DamageAssessment` from a base volley and an application factor. `fireControl.ts` and `droneApplication.ts` delegate to it; `app.ts` consumes the resulting `baseVolleyByType` from the view's `weaponAttacks` assessment when building missile launch specs, so the physical missile simulator receives a pre-scaled volley rather than reconstructing damage from per-shot values.

Cross-boundary contracts: `index.ts` exports the ewar domain types (`EwarLoadout`, `EwarProjection`, `CombatantConfig`, `DisruptionScriptSpec`, etc.), the weapon union (`WeaponSpec`, `TurretSpec`, `MissileSpec`, `DroneSpec`, `WeaponKind`), damage types (`DamageType`, `DamageVector`, `DamageResists`, `DefenseLayer`, `DefenseLayerSpec`, `DefenseSpec`, `RepairerSpec`, `DamageAssessment`, `TurretDamageBreakdown`, `MissileDamageBreakdown`, `DroneDamageBreakdown`), the damage vector helpers (`ZERO_DAMAGE`, `DAMAGE_TYPES`, `damageVectorSum`, `damageVectorScale`, `damageVectorAdd`, `damageVectorFromPartial`), `EwarResolver`, `EngagementEvaluator`, `EngagementFrameComposer`, `AttackState`, `AttackAssessment`, `EngagementInput`, `EngagementView`, `DroneApplication`, `WeaponDamageAssessor`, `StackingPenalty`, `StackingPenaltyImpl`, and `SimValueParser` for use by `fitting`, `app`, and `ui`. `StackingPenaltyImpl` is exported as a test seam so `fitting` and `tests` can instantiate the pure, stateless stacking penalty directly instead of duplicating its formula. `types.ts` hosts these shared DTOs. Weapon spec damage fields (`TurretSpec.damagePerShot`, `MissileSpec.damagePerMissile`, `DroneSpec.damagePerShot`) are `DamageVector` rather than scalar `number`; `DamageAssessment` carries `baseVolleyByType: DamageVector` (count-scaled, pre-application) alongside `appliedByType: DamageVector` and the scalar `appliedDps`/`application`/`volley` fields. `DamageType` was moved from `fitting/damageBreakdown.ts` to `sim/types.ts` as the single source of truth; `fitting` re-exports it from `sim`. `DefenseSpec`, `DefenseLayerSpec`, and `RepairerSpec` are the defense DTOs consumed by `fitting` (to produce) and `sim`/`ui` (to consume); they parallel `WeaponSpec` in the producer/consumer contract.

DI wiring: `module.ts` registers the read-model services (`simValueParser`, `hitChance`, `stackingPenalty`, `ewarResolver`, `turretBoosterResolver`, `missileBoosterResolver`, `sensorBoosterResolver`, `droneApplication`, `weaponDamageAssessor`, `engagementEvaluator`, `engagementFrameComposer`, `defenseAssessor`, `kinematics`, `missileApplication`, `rngFactory`), the `simWorldFactory`, the `live` (sampled) and `projection` (expected) `SimWorld` singletons built by it, and `engine` (the `EngagementEngine` singleton) against the singleton `container` in `src/container.ts`. The `simConfig` consumed by `simulation` is provided by the composition root. Sanctioned cross-boundary read models: `EwarResolver` (reach/potentials), `HitChance`, `SimValueParser`, `SensorBoosterResolver`, `DefenseAssessor`, `WeaponDamageAssessor` — these are pure read-model services consumed by `ui` and `appstate`; `missileApplication` is engine-internal (consumed by the missile simulator only) and no longer part of `SimCradle`. The engine and its sub-simulators are not imported outside `src/sim`.

Gate relaxed: `types.ts` and `index.ts` were removed from `no-new-exports` to add `TargetPainterSpec`, `MissileBoosterSpec`, `MissileBoosterLoadout`, `MissileBoosterActivation`, `MissileBoosterProjection`, and `PainterActivation` alongside the existing ewar/booster DTO family. These types are shared DTOs consumed by `fitting`, `app`, and `ui`; splitting them into a separate file would fragment the cross-boundary contract model. `fireControl.ts`, `cradle.ts`, and `module.ts` were removed to wire `DroneApplication` and `DroneSimulator` into the engagement evaluator and DI container; the evaluator dispatches drone weapons alongside turret and missile weapons, and the simulator tracks per-group drone flight state. `engagementFrameComposer.ts` was removed to add `WeaponAttack` alongside `EngagementView` — per-weapon assessments are needed by the UI to render Applied DPS source attribution. `DroneMode`, `DroneRuntimeState`, `DroneSimulator`, and `DroneSimConfig` were added to `types.ts` and `index.ts` as cross-boundary DTOs consumed by `app` and `ui`. `MissileApplicationResult`, `MissileRuntimeState`, `MissileAttackFacts`, `MissileLaunchSpec`, and `MissileSimConfig` were added to `types.ts` and `index.ts` as cross-boundary DTOs for the physical missile simulator; `MissileApplication.compute` was refactored to a pure formula (no frame/range) returning `MissileApplicationResult`, with `inRange`/`timeToImpact` moving to `MissileAttackFacts` sourced from the simulator. `AttackState` was extended with `missileFacts?: MissileAttackFacts` and `EngagementInput` was extended with `missileFacts` so the evaluator consumes physical missile facts from the simulator instead of computing analytical DPS; `assessMissile` requires `missileFacts` (the analytical fallback was removed). The missile simulator predicts interceptability, time to impact, and application from its own pursuit flight model — a hypothetical launch simulated step-by-step against linearly extrapolated target motion — so the analytic distance/flight-range bridge no longer feeds assessment. `defenseAssessment.ts` was added to `no-new-exports` with its initial exports (`DefenseAssessor`, `DefenseAssessment`, `LayerEhp`, `EMPTY_DEFENSE_ASSESSMENT`) re-exported through `index.ts` for consumption by `fitting` and `ui`; it parallels the existing per-side assessment pattern. `defenseSimulator.ts` was removed from `no-new-exports` to add `RepairMode`, `RepairerViewState`, `RahViewState`, `RepairerActivationEntry`, and `RahActivationEntry` as cross-boundary DTOs consumed by `app` and `ui` for Phase 4 active defense control; `RahSpec` was added to `types.ts` alongside the existing defense DTOs. `index.ts` was removed from `no-new-exports` to add `WeaponDamageAssessor` as a cross-boundary contract consumed by `SimCradle` (which is exported to `app` and `ui`); `DamageAssessment` was extended with `baseVolleyByType: DamageVector` (count-scaled, pre-application volley) so physical consumers can use a pre-scaled volley instead of recomputing damage from per-shot values, and `MissileLaunchSpec` was extended with the same field so the missile simulator receives the pre-scaled volley at launch time.

Gate relaxed (continued): `types.ts` and `index.ts` were further relaxed to add `ActiveOffensiveModule` as a cross-boundary DTO consumed by `ui/controls/portraits`. `TurretSpec`, `MissileSpec`, and `DroneSpec` now carry a required `moduleId: TypeId` — weapon identity is part of the domain value, consistent with all ewar/booster/repairer specs. `AppliedEwarEffect` evolved from a flat interface into a discriminated union carrying resolved numeric attribution per family (speed multiplier for web/grappler, tracking/optimal/falloff multipliers for disruptor, scan resolution/targeting range multipliers for dampener, signature multiplier for painter); `EwarEffectFamily` extended with `"painter"`. `EwarResolver.appliedEffects()` now covers target painters and returns the resolved attribution. `EwarResolver.disruptionMultipliers()` was added to expose stacked tracking/optimal/falloff multipliers directly, eliminating the synthetic `unitTurret` pattern in `ewarResolver.ts` and `ewarEffectDescriber.ts`. `EngagementView` extended with `incomingOffensiveModules: Record<Side, readonly ActiveOffensiveModule[]>` — target-indexed, composed by `EngagementFrameComposerImpl` from active weapon attacks (`appliedDps > 0`) and applied ewar effects. The portrait controller consumes this view field directly instead of re-deriving ewar activity in the UI layer.

Gate relaxed (inflicted DPS projection): the window-based inflicted meter was replaced by shadow-world forward projection. `restorable.ts` is new and exports the `Restorable<State>` seam (`capture`/`restore`) implemented by every stateful sub-simulator; its `State` DTOs (`SimulationState`, `LockClockState`, `DroneSimulatorState` with its group/body snapshots, `MissileSimulatorState` with its side/body snapshots, `WeaponClockState` with its cooldown snapshots, `DefenseSimulatorState` with its pool/repairer/rah snapshots) are exported from their owning files solely as data contracts for the projection restore and for test mocks — captured states are cloned on both capture and restore so they can be restored repeatedly while the shadow world mutates its own copies. `hitRoll.ts` exports `HitRollStrategy`, `sampledHitRoll`, and `expectedHitRoll` so the projection world runs the same `WeaponClockImpl` in expectation mode (the expected roll consumes no RNG draws, so `WeaponClockState` intentionally does not capture RNG state and `restore` rebuilds rngs from the seed); `rollHit` stays file-private behind the `_rollHit` test seam. `weaponClock.ts` gained a `hitRoll` constructor injection alongside those snapshot exports. `defenseSimulator.ts` no longer carries the rolling window (`setInflictedWindow`, buckets, `DefenseView.inflictedDps` removed); it accumulates cumulative per-layer `inflictedTotals()` and the DPS division moved to the engine, which divides the projected layer delta by the horizon into `InflictedDps` (type retained in `types.ts` for the UI). `engagementEngine.ts` now consumes two `SimWorld` bundles (`live` sampled, `projection` expected) instead of individual sub-simulators and re-projects on a 0.5 s cadence or when `update` marks the projection dirty; user actions are automatically reflected because every projection starts from fresh live captures. Because the defense tick-release buffers damage events up to the next 1 s boundary, `projectInflicted` calls `DefenseSimulator.flushPendingDamage()` after restoring the live captures and again after the horizon loop, so the delta of raw `inflictedTotals()` counts exactly the damage generated inside the projection window regardless of buffer phase. `simWorld.ts` is new and exports `SimWorld`, `SimWorldFactory`, and `SimWorldFactoryImpl` (registered in `module.ts`, which now registers `live` and `projection` singletons and drops the individual sub-simulator registrations — sub-simulators are constructed inside the factory); `index.ts` exports `SimWorld`/`SimWorldFactory` for `SimCradle`.

Gate relaxed (spool-up): `types.ts` hosts `TurretSpoolSpec`, `spoolMultiplier(spool, cycles)`, and `TurretDamageBreakdown.spoolFactor` as the spool-up contract. The disintegrator mechanism is single-source-of-truth at the assessment layer: `WeaponDamageAssessor.assess` gained an optional `rawDamageMultiplier` that scales the base volley at the source, so every derived field (`volley`, `nominalDps`, `appliedByType`, `appliedVolleyByType`, `appliedDps`) is spool-inclusive and `application` stays a pure ratio. `fireControl.assessTurret` computes `spoolFactor = spoolMultiplier(effectiveTurret.spool, spoolCycles)` from the runtime cycle count, keeps the spool-inclusive nominal DPS for a deactivated (out-of-optimal) disintegrator while zeroing application, and reports both `spoolFactor` and `inOptimal` on `TurretDamageBreakdown`. `WeaponClock` owns only spool state: `WeaponCooldown.spoolCycles` increments per completed cycle, resets on deactivation (cooldown entry deleted while a spooling turret's target is beyond optimal), lock loss, or weapon-signature change, and is exposed through `spoolCycles(side, weaponIndex)` for the engine; `rollEvent` no longer applies any spool math — events carry the assessment's spool-inclusive volley plus only the hit-quality roll. Following the `missileFacts` precedent, `EngagementInput` gained `spoolCycles: Record<Side, readonly number[]>` (built by `EngagementEngineImpl` from the clock accessor before compose, since compose precedes `weaponClock.step` in `runStep`) and `AttackState` gained `spoolCycles?: number`; `EngagementFrameComposerImpl` threads the per-weapon-index value into the evaluator.
