# moduleSlots

Module-to-slot mapping generated from EVE Online inventory categories. The public surface is `ModuleSlotCatalog` (exported from `index.ts`) and `StaticModuleSlotCatalog`. The data file `moduleSlots.ts` is generated by `scripts/generate-module-slots.ts` and emits two tables: `MODULE_SLOTS_BY_NAME` (used by the EFT parser for raw-text classification) and `MODULE_SLOTS_BY_ID` (used by the runtime catalog for id-based slot lookup). Both tables, the `ModuleSlot` type, and the catalog/index exports are gated by no-new-exports.
